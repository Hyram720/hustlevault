Refund Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

7-Day Money-Back Guarantee. If you're not satisfied with a digital product (such as an ebook) or an AI tool purchase for any reason, contact us within 7 days of your purchase date for a full refund.

What This Covers

  • Digital products and ebooks: one-time purchases are covered by the 7-day guarantee above.
  • Pay-per-use AI tools: each one-time tool purchase is covered by the same 7-day guarantee. Once a purchase is refunded, its results are no longer available.
  • Sponsor subscriptions: you can cancel anytime. Cancelling stops future billing, and your listing stays up until the end of the period you've paid for. We don't give partial refunds for unused time unless we approve one, and no refund is due for the current period if an ad is removed for breaking our Ad Policy.
  • VentureCheck: VentureCheck runs on its own website with its own checkout; its own refund terms apply to purchases made there.

How to Request a Refund

Email sunmarketingllc@gmail.com within 7 calendar days of your purchase with:

  • The email address used at checkout
  • The name of the product or tool purchased
  • Your order/receipt number (found in your purchase confirmation email)

No lengthy explanation required — a brief reason helps us improve, but is optional. Every refund is reviewed and approved by hand.

Processing Time

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.

After the 7-Day Window

Because our products and tool results are digital and delivered instantly, refund requests made more than 7 days after purchase are not eligible. All sales become final at that point.

Bundles

Ebook bundles are refundable in full within the same 7-day window. Partial refunds of individual items within a bundle are not offered.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a dispute with your bank — we resolve nearly all issues faster and without hassle. Chargebacks filed without first requesting a refund may result in loss of access to your purchases.

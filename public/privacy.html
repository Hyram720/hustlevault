Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hustle Vault ("we," "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use hustlevault.app (the "Site"), including our digital products, our AI tools, our sponsor program, and our emails.

Information We Collect

  • Purchase information: when you buy a digital product (such as an ebook) or a pay-per-use AI tool, the name and email address you give at checkout, and records of what you bought and when.
  • Payment information: all payments, including one-time purchases and sponsor subscriptions, are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
  • AI tool answers: the answers you type into one of our AI tools, and the results we generate for you. These are stored with your purchase so you can open your results again.
  • Sponsor information: if you ask about sponsoring or become a sponsor, your name, business name, email, website, the business or offer you're promoting, your message, and the logo, offer text and link you give us for your listing.
  • Newsletter information: if you sign up for our email newsletter, your email address, your name if you choose to give it, and a record of your consent.
  • Usage data: basic, first-party counts of visits, tool page views and clicks (with the page and any campaign tag in the link), plus a one-way hashed version of your IP address used only to stop abuse. Our host also processes standard request data such as IP address and browser type.

How We Use It

  • To deliver your purchases, generate your AI tool results, and send order confirmations and access links
  • To run sponsor listings: review and publish them, bill monthly subscriptions, and send you private links to your sponsor stats and to edit your listing
  • To send the newsletter, if you signed up for it
  • To provide customer support and process refunds
  • To improve the Site and prevent fraud and abuse

Sponsors

Sponsor slots ($100/month) are billed monthly through Stripe. When your listing is set up, we email you two private links: one to your stats (how often your logo was seen and clicked) and one to edit your listing. Anyone with those links can use them, so please keep them private. Impressions and clicks are counted without cookies, and known bots are filtered out.

Emails

We send emails through Resend. Transactional emails, such as purchase confirmations and the welcome email with your sponsor links, are sent because you bought something or signed up as a sponsor. If you sign up for our newsletter, we ask you to confirm your email address first (double opt-in), and every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link. You can unsubscribe at any time.

AI Tools

Our AI tools are listed on the Site: VentureCheck and the AI Offer Builder. When a tool runs, your answers are sent to OpenAI for processing through the Netlify AI Gateway, and may be checked by an automated safety filter. Please don't enter other people's personal details into the tools.

VentureCheck runs on its own website, with its own checkout and its own privacy policy. When you click through to VentureCheck, that site's policy applies to the information you give it.

We also run an internal pricing-monitoring tool used only by our own team. It checks public pricing pages of websites we choose, stores the prices it finds and any changes, and emails alerts to our team. It does not collect information about Site visitors.

What We Don't Do

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers needed to operate the Site: Stripe (payments and billing), Resend (email delivery), Netlify (hosting, data storage and AI processing) and OpenAI (AI processing). Each one is bound to use it only to provide their service to us.

Cookies

The Site itself doesn't set tracking cookies and doesn't use third-party analytics or advertising trackers. We use your browser's local storage to remember a few things on your device: the campaign tag you arrived with, links to your past AI tool purchases, notes you save in the Door Script tool, whether you closed the sponsor bar, and a sign-in token for our team's admin pages. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout pages to process payments securely and prevent fraud. You can clear site data or block cookies in your browser settings; the Site will still work, though checkout may be affected.

Data Retention

We keep purchase and billing records as long as needed for accounting, tax, and support purposes. You may request deletion of your data (subject to records we're legally required to keep) by emailing us.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., California, EU/UK), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, and to opt out of marketing. To exercise any of these rights, email sunmarketingllc@gmail.com.

Children

The Site is not directed to anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are effective when posted here with an updated date.

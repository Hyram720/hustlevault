## PART 2: THE 7-DOCUMENT CLOSING PACKET CHECKLIST

### Document 1: Stock Purchase & Clean-Entity Warranty Agreement
- [ ] Identifies the exact Wyoming C-Corporation (`Name, Filing ID, Incorporation Date`) and transfers **100% of the issued and outstanding capital stock (`100,000 Common Shares`)** from Seller to Buyer.
- [ ] **Seller's Clean-Entity Warranty:** Seller warrants that prior to the Closing Timestamp, the Corporation is in Active Good Standing with the State of Wyoming, has zero debts, zero UCC liens, zero pending or threatened litigation, zero undisclosed contracts, and (for Track A) has never applied for or obtained an IRS EIN.
- [ ] **Buyer's Active-Operation Representation & Indemnification:** Buyer represents that Buyer is acquiring 100% control of the Corporation to actively manage and operate Buyer's own business (not as a passive investment security), acknowledges no loan/funding guarantees have been made by Seller, and agrees to **indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Seller** from any and all claims, taxes, debts, bank accounts, or liabilities arising on or after the Closing Timestamp.

### Document 2: Bill of Sale & Irrevocable Stock Power
- [ ] Transfers title to the `100,000 Common Shares` and all bundled digital assets (matching `.com` domain, 6-page website files, Corporate Identity Kit, and Corporate Binder) free and clear of all encumbrances.

### Document 3: Cancellation of Stock Certificate #1 & Issuance of Stock Certificate #2
- [ ] Original `Stock Certificate #1` (issued to Seller for `100,000 Common Shares`) marked **"CANCELLED - TRANSFERRED TO CERTIFICATE #2 ON [CLOSING DATE]"** in the Corporate Stock Ledger.
- [ ] New **`Stock Certificate #2`** issued in Buyer's full legal name for **`100,000 Common Shares (100% of Issued & Outstanding Shares)`**, signed and sealed.

### Document 4: Written Resignation of Seller & Unanimous Joint Resolution of Director/Shareholder
- [ ] **Resignation of Initial Incorporator, Officer & Director:** Signed by Seller, resigning immediately and unconditionally as President, Secretary, Treasurer, Director, and Signer effective as of the Closing Timestamp, with an explicit statement that Seller retains **zero nominee, officer, director, or beneficial ownership status**.
- [ ] **Unanimous Resolution Electing New Sole Director & Officers:** Elects Buyer as Sole Director, President, Secretary, and Treasurer effective immediately.

### Document 5: Immediate WyoBiz Secretary of State Officer/Director Update & Registered Agent Transfer
- [ ] Logged into `wyobiz.wyo.gov` during closing and updated the public corporate officer/director record to remove Seller's name and record Buyer as President, Secretary, Treasurer, and Director.
- [ ] Transferred the Wyoming Commercial Registered Agent sub-account billing and mail-forwarding profile to Buyer's email and phone number.
- [ ] Downloaded the updated WyoBiz filing receipt and fresh **Certificate of Good Standing**.

### Document 6: IRS Tax ID (EIN) Handoff Protocol (Track A vs. Track B)
- [ ] **IF TRACK A (Virgin / No Prior EIN):** Guided Buyer to `irs.gov` after Document 4 & 5 were completed so Buyer obtained a brand-new IRS EIN in 5 minutes listing **Buyer's own name and SSN as the IRS Responsible Party**.
- [ ] **IF TRACK B (Existing EIN & 80-Paydex Profile):**
  1. Delivered original IRS CP 575 EIN Confirmation Letter and copies of all prior `$0`-activity Form 1120 tax returns.
  2. Delivered official **Bank Account Closure Confirmation Letter** proving Seller's temporary checking account was closed at `$0.00` balance prior to closing.
  3. Both Seller and Buyer executed **IRS Form 8822-B (`Change of Address or Responsible Party - Business`)** naming Buyer as the new Responsible Party (with Buyer's SSN).
  4. Seller mailed a signed copy of **Form 8822-B via USPS Certified Mail (Return Receipt Requested)** to the IRS Service Center and saved the certified mail receipt in Seller's permanent compliance archive.

### Document 7: Digital Infrastructure & Credit Bureau Credential Handover Receipt
- [ ] Pushed the matching `.com` domain to Buyer's Cloudflare/Namecheap account (or provided EPP transfer code).
- [ ] Transferred the 6-page Netlify corporate website deployment and updated Contact/Leadership page details if requested by Buyer.
- [ ] Transferred `@companydomain.com` administrator email credentials (and prompted Buyer to rotate password).
- [ ] *(Track B Only):* Updated D&B `iUpdate`, Experian Business, and the 5 Tier-1 Net-30 vendor account portals with Buyer's contact details.
